Chapel Youth Fellowship

CYF is the youth arm of the Chapel of Faith Interdenominational Church, Federal University of Technology, Akure (FUTA).

The vision of CYF is to build a living fellowship where we all grow in the knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, academic excellence abounds, service to God is rendered in love and unity even as we reach out to the lost and the unsaved, restoring them to the Father. Her mission is to raise well equipped followers of Christ through: 1. Preaching. (2 Timothy 4: 2) 2. Teaching. (Matthew 28: 19-20) 3. Bible Study. (2 Timothy 2:15, 2 Timothy 3 16-17) 4. Discipleship. (1 Corinthians 11:1, Acts 18: 24-28) 5. Evangelism. (Mark 16: 15-16) 6. Prayer meetings. (Matthew 18: 19-20, James 5:16b) 7. Academic Tutorials. (Proverbs 9: 9
